## CE-0231 — Diagnostic scale horizon suppressed every global solve

**Observed/fixed:** the Stage-5 early-kill trace had 13,306 decisions and no
global publication. Player projection was not the blocker: 12,594 decisions
had exact zero-lag projection and 712 had exact one-step projection. The
diagnostic constant-scale schedule covered 120 frames, while the initial

maximum sensor age, async lead, and kernel horizon (269 frames), and each

is unknown-direction, resulting policies are queryable shadow evidence only
and cannot constrain physical actions. A rotated physical gate must confirm
actual submit, completion, publication, and query.
