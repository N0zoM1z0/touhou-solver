# TH08 Counterexamples CE-0220–CE-0269

## CE-0220: Item ledger retained over-cap Power and sorted away native active order

Date: 2026-07-31

Observed symptom:

- The deterministic item test expected a large Power pickup at Power 124 to
  produce Power 132.
- `ItemPoolState` always selected the lowest free slot and
  `step_item_pool()` updated active items in numeric slot order.
- Full-Power conversion changed only item type, omitting the native velocity
  clamp.

Invalid assumption:

- `add_player_power(+8)` is the terminal native write even when the result
  crosses 128.
- Numeric pool index is the item manager's execution order.
- Converting an active Power item to overflow changes only its value/type and
  cannot affect its later same-frame motion or pickup.

Evidence or trace:

- `add_player_power` at `0x00441850` first performs the integer addition into
  the float Power field;
- small and large pickup branches at `0x00440D5F` and `0x004411D8` call
  `0x00406FA0(128)` immediately after the result reaches or crosses 128;
- allocation at `0x004400BD..0x004401D1` advances a persistent cyclic
  2,096-slot cursor;
- allocation appends at `0x00440413..0x0044043E`, while update starts from
  the active linked-list head at `0x0044051C`; and
- conversion at `0x00441450` changes other active type-0/2 items to type 8
  and, when `vy > -0.5`, writes `(vx, vy) = (0, -0.5)`.

Small deterministic falsifier:

- resources: Power 124;
- item: one collected large Power item;
- old model result: Power 132;
- shipped/native-correct result: Power 128, effective delta 4, full-Power
  conversion enabled.

Ordering falsifier:

- active list `(slot 10 large Power, slot 2 small Power)` at Power 120;
- slot 10 reaches 128 first and converts slot 2 to overflow before slot 2's
  update;
- sorting by slot would process slot 2 first and create a different pickup,
  score, motion, and resource ledger.

Root-cause class: world model / native container and resource semantics.

Correction:

- clamp small/large pickup results to canonical Power 128;
- retain the rotating allocation cursor and explicit active linked-list order;
- reject out-of-range spawns before cursor/RNG mutation; and
- apply the full-Power type and conditional velocity conversion before later
  active-list items update.

Regression test:

- Power 124 plus large Power equals 128, not 132;
- the `(10, 2)` active-order fixture collects large Power then overflow;
- a cursor at 10 allocates slot 10 even when lower slots are free;
- out-of-range spawn preserves cursor and RNG; and
- already-updated versus not-yet-updated converted items retain their
  different same-frame motion outcomes.

Live verification: no game or controller process was run. Shipped
instruction/dataflow semantics are revalidated; the corrected model has
offline implementation authority only.

Status: fixed in the item model, pool recurrence, tests, IDA comments, and
static capability ledger. No collection policy or route-survival authority is
promoted.
