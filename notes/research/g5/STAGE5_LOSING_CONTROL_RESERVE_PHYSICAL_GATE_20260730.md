## Offline Versus Physical Evidence

Trace replay is useful because it reuses exact recorded hazards, player state,
delay support, and policy guidance to reject candidates that already worsen
the hard vector. It is still a single-decision counterfactual over a recorded
future. It cannot propagate the changed action through native RNG, shooting,
enemy death, bullet birth, or later observations.

Native replay, when naturally available, is stronger for reconstructing the
same recorded physical history and testing observer/model hypotheses. It
still replays recorded input and is not evidence that a new policy would have
survived. Fresh original-game play remains the acceptance gate.
